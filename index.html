The Curriculum

Five modules. Built with Bold Idea at the table.

OptimaNova brings a curriculum framework built specifically for youth after-school programs. We adapt it to Bold Idea's structure, timeline, and student experience. The result is a program that feels native to Bold Idea because it was designed with Bold Idea at the table, not handed down from outside.

Module 01
AI Foundations
How AI works, in plain language students and mentors can teach and use. LLMs, image models, tools. Demystifying the technology so it becomes a resource, not a mystery.
Module 02
Prompt Design
Prompt engineering built for student projects. Context, iteration, multi-modal output. Students learn to direct AI rather than be directed by it.
Module 03
Responsible AI
Bias, data privacy, digital consent, and ethics. Built for the age group and grounded in the real world Bold Idea students are navigating every day.
Module 04
AI-Assisted Creation
Students apply AI to the projects they are already building: websites, games, apps. AI becomes part of the creation process, not a distraction from it.
Module 05
Careers in AI
Who builds AI, who uses it, and what the jobs look like. Connected to Bold Idea's existing career exploration work and the Dallas tech sector.
Built In
Mentor-ready from day one
Bold Idea's mentors are tech professionals, not full-time educators. Every session guide, prompt library, and reference tool is designed so a mentor can pick it up, walk in, and deliver confidently without prep time or an AI background.
04
The Licensing Model

Why a license beats a binder.

Here is the uncomfortable truth about AI curriculum: the moment it is finished, it starts aging. The tools students will use this fall did not exist eighteen months ago. A curriculum purchased once is current for a semester, familiar for a year, and a liability by year three.

A license flips that burden. Keeping the curriculum current is our job, not yours. Bold Idea's lean staff stays focused on students, mentors, and sites while OptimaNova carries the updates, the new materials, and the retraining, every single year. The license covers every Bold Idea site, program, mentor, and student. One license. No per-seat fees.

Every Year
Annual curriculum refresh.
Every module reviewed and updated to current tools, models, and best practices before each program year begins. Students never learn from last year's AI.
Every Year
New content drops.
As AI capabilities shift, new exercises, prompt libraries, and session materials ship to every site. The curriculum grows as the field does.
Every Year
Mentor re-training.
An annual refresher session so new and returning mentors deliver with confidence. Mentor turnover stops being a curriculum risk.
Year-Round
Priority support.
A direct line to OptimaNova for staff and mentor questions throughout the program year. When something comes up at a site, you have a partner on call.

Implementation

Four weeks to launch. Current forever after.

A structured four-week implementation that builds the curriculum alongside your team, not for them. Bold Idea's program philosophy, student voice, and mentor experience are built into every module from week one. Then the license takes over and keeps it current, year after year.

Week 01
Discovery & Listening
OptimaNova goes deep on what Bold Idea has built. Surveys, interviews with program staff, mentors, and where possible, students. We learn what the curriculum already does well, what the secret sauce is, and how AI needs to fit around it. We also assess tools and tech access across sites so the curriculum reflects what mentors and students can actually use.
Staff Interviews · Curriculum Audit · Mentor Surveys · Tool Assessment
Week 02
Build the Curriculum
Using our proven AI curriculum framework as the shell, we build the Bold Idea version. Every module mapped to your existing session structure, your grade range, and your mentor model. Quizzes, prompt exercises, reference materials, and facilitator guides developed in parallel. Bold Idea reviews in real time and shapes what we build as we go.
Module Development · Facilitator Guides · Student Exercises · Prompt Libraries
Week 03
Review & Refine
Bold Idea's team sees the full curriculum live for the first time, walked through session by session. Staff and mentors test the materials, flag what needs adjustment, and validate that the content speaks in Bold Idea's voice. We revise. The curriculum goes through Bold Idea's lens before it goes anywhere else.
Live Walkthrough · Staff Feedback · Mentor Review · Revisions
Week 04
Train the Trainers
A half-day working session, three to six hours, in person or virtual, where OptimaNova equips Bold Idea's staff and mentors to deliver the new curriculum with confidence. Not a lecture. A working session where they practice it. They leave ready to teach. We leave knowing they do not need us in the room to make it work.
Staff Training · Mentor Certification · Delivery Practice · Resource Handoff
Every Year
The License Cycle
Implementation is the beginning, not the deliverable. Each program year, OptimaNova refreshes every module, ships new materials as AI evolves, re-trains your mentors, and stays on call for your team. Bold Idea never teaches from last year's AI, and never carries the burden of keeping up.
